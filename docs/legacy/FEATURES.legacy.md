# Music Manager — Standout Features Catalog & Implementation Map

This document catalogues the comprehensive inventory of Namida's standout features that generic music players lack, structured for implementation in our MIT-licensed hybrid player. *(Advanced DSP/sound effect features are excluded as low-priority, while Smart Transitions are fully retained).*

---

## 1. Comprehensive Inventory of Namida Standout Features

### 1.1 Visuals & Atmosphere
* **Dynamic Palette Background**: Automatically generates smooth, multi-stop ambient gradient meshes derived from the dominant colors of the active album cover.
* **Waveform Seekbar**: Replaces flat horizontal scrubbers with an animated audio waveform visualization displaying peaks, drops, and energy levels.
* **Animated Backgrounds & Visualizers**: Floating ambient particles, glassmorphic backdrop filters (`backdrop-blur-md`), and optional custom background wallpapers.
* **Widescreen & Carousel Layouts**: Adaptive widescreen desktop layout with animated cover art carousels and smooth album transitions.
* **Specialized Curated Views**: Filter by Moods, Custom Tags, Star Ratings, Song Languages, and detailed personal Listening Stats (total listen time, most played artists).

### 1.2 Lyrics Subsystem
* **Multi-Source Synced Lyrics**: Real-time line-synced lyrics with automated fallback querying (LRCLIB, Musixmatch, Genius, NetEase).
* **Manual Lyrics Timing Offset Slider**: On-screen timing adjuster (`+` / `-` milliseconds) that immediately shifts lyric scroll position to lock timing if the lyrics lead or lag the audio.
* **Floating Desktop & In-Video Overlay**: Transparent always-on-top desktop lyrics widget, as well as an integrated lyric overlay directly on top of playing video files.
* **Multi-Language Romanization**: Automatic transliteration of non-Latin lyrics (Japanese Romaji/Kana, Korean Hangul, Chinese Pinyin, Thai) displayed above or below original characters.
* **Offline Lyrics Caching**: Automatically saves fetched `.lrc` files locally alongside tracks or in an indexed local SQLite/IndexedDB store.

### 1.3 Unified Audio & Video Integration
* **Instant Audio / Video Toggle**: Dedicated 1-click button on the player bar to toggle between audio-only mode (album art display) and official music video playback on the fly.
* **Unified Queue**: Mix local `.mp3`, `.flac`, `.opus`, and `.mp4` video files within the same active playlist and playback queue.
* **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Pop-out video player window that floats above all other desktop applications while continuing playback.

### 1.4 Smart Transitions
* **Equal-Power Smart Crossfade**: Smoothly blends the end of the outgoing song into the beginning of the incoming song over a configurable duration (1 to 10 seconds).
* **Gapless Playback**: Pre-buffers upcoming audio tracks to eliminate gaps and clicks between live recordings or continuous concept albums.
* **Play / Pause Smooth Fade**: Fades audio volume down over 200ms when paused, and fades up over 200ms when resumed, avoiding abrupt pops.
* **Skip Silence**: Detects intro and outro silence thresholds (e.g. below $-50\text{dBFS}$) and automatically fast-forwards to the start of audible sound.
* **ReplayGain Loudness Normalization**: Automatically balances volume between quiet acoustic songs and loud mastered tracks.

### 1.5 Library & Tagging Management
* **Batch Tag Editor**: Edit Title, Artist, Album, Year, Track Number, and embed Cover Art directly into local files.
* **Multi-Artist & Genre Separators**: Intelligently parses collaboration strings using customizable delimiters (e.g. `feat.`, `ft.`, `&`, `;`, `,`) into distinct clickable artist entities rather than a single lumped string.
* **Folder Exclusion & Duplicate Prevention**: Exclude specific system folders (e.g., ringtones, voice memos) and prevent duplicate files based on audio hashing or title/artist matching.

### 1.6 System Integration & Extras
* **Discord Rich Presence**: Broadcasts active track title, artist name, elapsed/remaining time, and high-resolution album cover directly to Discord status.
* **Backup & Restore**: Export and import complete library configurations, custom tags, ratings, and playlists as human-readable JSON files.
